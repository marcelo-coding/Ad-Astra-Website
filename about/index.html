About

How we run accounts

TACoS on our flagship accounts stays under 15 percent because the daily bid work in our accounts runs as code, not guesswork. We are Ad Astra, an Amazon advertising agency run by operators who build their own software with ten years in Amazon e-commerce. Across Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, Amazon DSP, and AMC, we run 15 to 20 accounts at a time.

Every account, tool, hour, and routine is a system we measure and improve. Our best work hits problems that span a whole account or a whole company, not isolated tactics. Teams come back for the mental models, because a model tells you what to do next quarter while a trick only tells you what to do today. The daily work keeps graduating into software for that reason. Any decision We have made the same way three times is one we would rather encode once and audit forever.

Our Story

We started on the seller side and helped take a product launch to $3M in revenue within its first ten months. From there the work moved into portfolio management across 15 to 20 concurrent accounts. We now run PPC, DSP, and the automation that protects margin.

Certifications and Credentials

Our certifications include Amazon Ads Certified in Sponsored Ads. We practice Amazon DSP and AMC on live accounts, and we open sourced the engines that do our daily work, because code you can read is a stronger credential than any badge.

Our Stack

The stack behind our accounts includes the SKAG optimization engine, the data extractor that feeds it, a fleet of Amazon Ads MCP servers, the Cloud MCP Admin control plane, and the AI agent workflows that run on top of them all.
