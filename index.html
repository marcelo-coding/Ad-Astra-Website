About

The team behind the dashboards

Over the last ten years inside Amazon e-commerce we worked first on the seller side and then as a dedicated advertising team. Along the way we helped take a product launch to $3M in revenue within its first ten months, and that experience taught us that growth only counts when the margin survives it.

Today we manage advertising for 15 to 20 accounts at any given time across Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, Amazon DSP, and AMC. Across that portfolio we hold TACoS under 15 percent on our flagship accounts because we refuse to do repetitive work by hand. The bid rules, the report pipelines, and the campaign hygiene in our accounts all run as code that we wrote, versioned, and published.

If you want an advertising partner who treats your budget like an engineering problem with a paper trail, we will probably get along. The fastest way to confirm that is a short call where we look at your account together and we tell you exactly what we would change first.
